Our Mission

The Pact

Grassroots live music is at the heart of every scene worth talking about. But getting a gig off the ground — finding the crowd, selling the tickets, making the numbers work — is harder than it should be for independent artists and promoters.

Pactd exists to change that. We give organisers the tools to create and sell tickets directly, and we give fans a single place to discover what's happening in their local scene.

Every ticket sold through Pactd is a commitment — a pact — between the artist and the audience. Your ticket doesn't just buy a seat. It makes the night happen.

What Pactd Does

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Discover Nearby Gigs

Browse events by location and genre. Find out what's happening in your town tonight or plan ahead for upcoming shows.

Direct Ticketing

Organisers create events and sell tickets directly to fans. No middleman, no inflated fees eating into every sale.

Organiser Tools

Create events, manage capacity, edit details, and scan tickets at the door — all from the same app.

QR Ticket Scanning

Every ticket gets a unique QR code. Scan at the door for fast, reliable entry — no paper, no faff.

Event Notifications

Get reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before your gig. Never miss a show, and always know if anything changes.

Favourite Venues & Artists

Follow the organisers and venues you love. Build your personal scene and never miss a show from acts you care about.
