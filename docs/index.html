The Pact Model

Pactd's core innovation — a commitment-based ticketing model that removes financial risk from independent promoters.

Traditional Events

  • Organiser pays upfront regardless of turnout
  • Venue booked whether or not enough tickets sell
  • Attendees book and sometimes don't show
  • Artists play to empty rooms

The Pact Way

  • Organiser sets a minimum ticket threshold
  • Buyers commit — their purchase makes it real
  • Event proceeds only when threshold is met
  • Every seat filled. Every show matters.

For Everyone

  • Artists know they have a guaranteed audience
  • Venues see predictable attendance numbers
  • Fans know the gig is actually happening
  • Grassroots music thrives

Each event has a ticket threshold — the minimum number of tickets that must be sold for the gig to go ahead. Until that threshold is met, the event is shown as building momentum. Once it's reached, it's confirmed. That commitment is The Pact.

Event Discovery

The Discover tab is the heart of Pactd — a powerful, real-time event browser with smart filtering.

🔤

Full-Text Search

Search across event names, venues, and towns simultaneously.

📍

Location Filter

Filter by town. Results are automatically sorted by distance from your GPS location.

🎭

Category Filter

Music, Exhibition, Convention, Corporate, Comedy, Sports, or Other.

🎸

Genre Filter

16 music genres — appears only when "Music" category is selected. Alternative, Blues, Classical, Country, Electronic, Folk, Hip-Hop, Indie, Jazz, Metal, Pop, Punk, R&B, Reggae, Rock, Soul.

💰

Free / Paid

Toggle to show only free events or only paid events.

⭐ Favourites Section ▾

Shows all events you've manually favourited, plus every upcoming event from venues you've saved. This section always appears first when you have saved content, ensuring you never miss a gig from your favourite spots.

📅 This Week Carousel ▾

A horizontal scrolling carousel of events happening within the next 7 days — a quick glance at what's on imminently without having to scroll the full feed.

🗺️ By Location ▾

The main feed groups events by town and sorts towns by distance from your device's current location. Within each town, events are ordered by date. This makes it easy to find local gigs without knowing exactly which areas to search.

🚫 Sold Out ▾

Sold-out events are grouped into a separate section at the bottom of the feed. They remain visible for context and social proof — knowing an event sold out can drive interest in future shows from the same organiser.

Event Management

Everything an organiser needs to run a gig — from creation to post-show analytics.

➕ Creating an Event ▾

Fill in the event form with the following information:

Basic Info
  • Band / artist name
  • Venue (auto-filled for venue admins)
  • Date and time
  • Description
  • Event image
Ticketing
  • Total ticket quantity
  • Threshold (minimum to proceed)
  • Price + currency
  • Free event toggle
Classification
  • Category (Music, Comedy, etc.)
  • Music genre (if Music category)
Location
  • Address and town
  • GPS coordinates (auto-detected)
📱 Ticket Scanning at the Door ▾

The scanning screen has two modes:

📷 Scan Mode

Live camera view that reads a guest's QR code and instantly validates or rejects the ticket with visual feedback.

📋 List Mode

Full attendee list showing each ticket's status (Valid / Used / Refunded), live attendance counts, and percentage of seats filled.

📊 Sales Analytics ▾

Each event detail screen (for the organiser) includes a chart showing ticket sales over time. Track when demand spikes — after a social post, a press mention, or as the event date approaches — to better plan future events.

♻️ Duplicate Events ▾

Regular promoters can duplicate any past event to pre-fill the creation form with all the same details. Just update the date and you're ready to go — great for recurring nights or repeat bookings.

Tickets

From purchase to entry — the full ticket lifecycle on Pactd.

🛒

Purchasing

Free events: one-tap claim. Paid events: Stripe checkout with a 10% booking fee (minimum 50p). Apple Pay and Google Pay supported.

📲

QR Code Entry

Each ticket contains a unique QR code. Present it at the door for instant scanning by the organiser's app.

📅

Add to Calendar

One tap adds the event to your device's native calendar app with all the event details included.

🔀

Transfer Tickets

Generate a shareable transfer link. Recipients click the link to claim the ticket. Transfers are time-limited and can be cancelled before they're accepted.

🕐

Past Tickets

Toggle between upcoming and past tickets. Your gig history stays on your profile forever.

Payments & Payouts

Transparent, Stripe-powered payments for organisers and attendees.

💳

Stripe Checkout

Paid ticket purchases go through Stripe's secure checkout. Card details are never stored by Pactd.

📱

Apple Pay & Google Pay

Native wallet payments are supported for a frictionless checkout experience on compatible devices.

💰

Transparent Fees

A 10% booking fee (minimum 50p) is applied to paid events. Free events have zero platform fee.

🌍

Multi-Currency

Events can be priced in EUR, GBP, USD, CAD, AUD, or CHF.

Organisers connect a Stripe account to receive ticket revenue directly.

🔗

💵

Balance Dashboard

📈

Per-Event Earnings

Drill down into each event's financial breakdown: gross revenue, platform fee, and net earnings.

✅

Account Status

Clear indicators show whether your Stripe account has charges and payouts enabled, so you know when you're ready to start selling.
